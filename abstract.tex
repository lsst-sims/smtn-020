
\begin{abstract}
Over the ten years of the LSST, Rubin Observatory will acquire on the order of two million science observations. From twilight to twilight, except during bad weather or planned maintenance, the Simonyi Telescope will be choosing a new target every 30~seconds, with every observation contributing towards the LSST science goals.  Planning these observations is the work of the Rubin Feature Based Scheduler (FBS).  
The FBS has been generating simulated observations for over ten years, helping to improve the LSST survey strategy and the algorithms within the FBS, in a collaborative process between the project and the astronomical community under the guidance of the LSST Survey Cadence Optimization Committee (SCOC). While the FBS has been in active use with the Rubin AuxTel for several years building the framework for interactions between the FBS and summit system, only a subset of the FBS capabilities were necessary. 

During the commissioning of LSSTCam, the full capability of FBS was put into action, with commissioning configurations similar to the full survey configuration now in use. This configuration includes multiple modes of observing, which automatically adapt to telemetry about the observatory, night sky and weather conditions during the night, varying from targeted pre-planned Deep Drilling Field observations to wide-area observations designed to acquire pairs of visits every 30 minutes. The FBS will also appropriately respond to safety concerns such as lunar avoidance zones as well as respect configurable altitude or azimuth constraints. During commissioning, we tested these FBS configurations, adjusting the survey strategy, monitoring FBS performance and survey progress, as well as learning important lessons about the LSST scheduling, the characteristics of resulting observations, and interactions with data management processing. As part of the commissioning surveys, between May to September 2025 over 20,000 science visits were acquired, including over 13,000 designed to be similar to the Wide Fast Deep survey, just under 1000 visits designed to be like Deep Drilling Fields, and almost 200 visits to test ToO acquisition.
\end{abstract}
